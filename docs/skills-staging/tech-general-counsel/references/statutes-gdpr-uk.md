# GDPR and UK GDPR Digest

verified: 2026-10-15. Source: ICO guidance on contracts (Article 28), retrieved 2026-10-15. Applies only once an EEA or UK individual's data is processed; the `eea-uk` CONFIRM item decides whether this file is live.

## Article 28(3) processor contract: mandatory content

The contract must set out: subject matter and duration of processing, nature and purpose, types of personal data, categories of data subjects, and the controller's obligations and rights. It must include terms that the processor:

1. Acts only on the controller's documented instructions, unless law requires otherwise (and then tells the controller, unless prohibited).
2. Ensures persons processing the data are under a duty of confidence.
3. Takes appropriate security measures (Article 32).
4. Engages subprocessors only with prior authorization (general or specific) and under a written contract flowing down the same obligations.
5. Assists the controller in responding to data-subject rights requests.
6. Assists with security, breach notification, and data protection impact assessments, considering the nature of processing and information available.
7. At the end of the contract deletes or returns all personal data at the controller's choice and deletes existing copies unless law requires storage.
8. Makes available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance and submits to audits and inspections by the controller or its auditor.

Gap in our Terms 17.2 as of 2026-10-15: items 6 (DPIA assistance) and 8 (audits and inspections) are missing or partial. Add them in the next rewrite; TDPSA sec. 541.104 mirrors the same list and benefits from the fix.

## Transfers

EU Standard Contractual Clauses (2021/914) for EEA to US transfers; UK International Data Transfer Agreement or UK Addendum for UK transfers. Transfer impact assessment expected. The EU-US Data Privacy Framework is an alternative only for certified organizations; Echo 1 Labs is not certified.

## Article 27 representative

A controller or processor not established in the EEA (or UK) that offers goods or services to, or monitors, individuals there must designate a representative in-region unless processing is occasional, not large scale, and unlikely to risk rights. Trigger the appointment before marketing to or onboarding an EEA or UK customer. Privacy 12 already promises to publish the representative's details.

## Timelines

Data-subject requests: one month, extendable by two further months for complex or numerous requests with notice. Breach: controller to supervisory authority within 72 hours of awareness; processor to controller without undue delay. Our 72-hour processor commitment is stricter than required.

## Article 22

No decision based solely on automated processing producing legal or similarly significant effects without a lawful basis and safeguards including human intervention. Our HITL requirement is the safeguard; keep it mandatory, not optional, in both documents.
